FAQ

Questions owners ask.

Is this just AI-generated ads?

No. AI agents speed up research, writing and editing, but they work from your real website, photos and reviews, and nothing ships until a human has checked it and you've approved it.

Do I need professional photos or video?

No. Quorum starts with what's already on your website. Real photos of your crew and your trucks do even better, so send them if you have them.

Who actually runs the ads?

A human. Agents speed up research and production, but a person sets up, watches and adjusts every campaign.

How much should I spend on ads?

We set a test budget together on the call, based on your area and how many jobs you can take on. You can change it anytime.

Where does my ad money go?

Straight to Meta, on your own card. Quorum's fee is separate, so you can see exactly what the ads cost.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Local service businesses: HVAC, plumbing, roofing, electrical, landscaping and similar trades that win work from nearby homeowners.
