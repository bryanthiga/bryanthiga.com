Case study · Agency

Quorum

An ads team made of AI agents for local service businesses. The agents do the repeatable work of running Meta ads; a person signs off on everything that spends money.

Role
Designed, built and run solo
Stack
Node.js, Claude, Remotion
Status
Live
The problem

Small budgets, the same amount of work.

A landscaper or a cleaning company spending a few thousand dollars a month on Meta needs the same work as a bigger account: research, hooks, creative in every placement size, audiences, daily budget decisions and a report. An agency retainer usually costs more than the ad spend, and doing it yourself takes hours you don't have.

Most of that work is repeatable. The judgment calls are few, but they're the ones that matter: what you're allowed to claim, which ad gets more money, and when to stop. Quorum hands the repeatable parts to agents and keeps the judgment calls with a person.

Where it started

My own media-buying engine.

Before Quorum there was the automation engine I built for my own media buying. It launches creative, checks every campaign every two hours, and applies CPA guardrails that pause ad sets running over target, scale the ones holding under it, and reactivate paused ones.

103Node.js automation scripts
146hooks in a tested library
18angles
10creative styles

Quorum takes that engine and splits it into agents with one job each, so it can run for businesses other than mine.

How it works

Eight agents, one sign-off.

Each agent has a single job and produces something a person can check. Nothing touches the ad account until the review step is passed.

  1. ResearchReads the site and reviews, pulls the claims that can be proven, studies competitor ads.
  2. HooksWrites the opening lines, scores them and keeps the best three.
  3. CreativeCuts 15-second videos and stills from the client's photos, in every placement size.
  4. Audiences + campaignMaps the service area, keeps past customers out of prospecting, builds the campaign.
  5. Human reviewEvery ad, audience and budget change is checked by a person before it spends a dollar.
  6. Budget, testing, reportsPaces spend daily, runs hook against hook, writes the weekly report.

The agents

Who does what.

  1. 01 · ResearchReads the client's site and reviews, pulls the claims that can be proven, studies competitor ads.
  2. 02 · HooksWrites the opening lines that stop the scroll, scores them, keeps the best three.
  3. 03 · CreativeCuts 15-second videos and stills from the client's own photos, in their colours.
  4. 04 · AudiencesMaps the service area and keeps past customers out of prospecting.
  5. 05 · CampaignBuilds the campaign in the client's ad account, then waits for an OK.
  6. 06 · BudgetPaces spend daily and shifts money toward the ads that bring in calls.
  7. 07 · TestingRuns hook against hook, cuts losers early, scales winners.
  8. 08 · ReportsTracks calls and leads and writes a plain-English weekly summary.
Design decisions

Agents draft. People decide.

  • One job per agent. A narrow job gives an output you can check. One large prompt doing everything is hard to review and hard to fix.
  • Only what's true. Every claim in an ad comes from the client's website or from the client. Reviews are quoted word for word.
  • A person approves first. No ad, audience or budget change goes live without a human sign-off.
  • The client's money stays in the client's account. Ad spend goes straight to Meta on their card.
  • A website is the whole brief. No intake forms or onboarding deck. The research agent starts from the site.
