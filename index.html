01  /  What Quorum does

An ads department for businesses that don't have one.

Quorum runs Facebook and Instagram ads for local service businesses. Eight AI subagents do the research, writing, editing, campaign building and reporting. A media buyer checks every piece and signs off before a dollar of yours is spent. You get the output of a full ad team without hiring one.

// 01

Made from what's true

Every ad is built from your own website, your photos and your real reviews. No stock smiles, no made-up numbers, no claims you can't back up.

// 02

More creative, tested faster

The agents do the hours of production. That means several hooks for every offer, fresh ads every month, and losers cut before they waste your budget.

// 03

A human on the hook

Agents don't spend your money. A person who buys media for a living sets up, watches and adjusts every campaign, and answers your messages.

07  /  FAQ

    
Questions owners ask.

    

      
Is this just AI-generated ads?
No. AI agents speed up research, writing and editing, but they work from your real website, photos and reviews, and nothing ships until a media buyer has checked it and you've approved it.

      
Do I need professional photos or video?
No. I start with what's already on your website. Real photos of your crew and your trucks do even better, so send them if you have them.

      
Who actually runs the ads?
I do. Agents speed up research and production, but a working media buyer sets up, watches and adjusts every campaign.

      
How much should I spend on ads?
We'll set a test budget together on the call, based on your area and how many jobs you can take on. You can change it anytime.

      
Where does my ad money go?
Straight to Meta, on your own card. My fee is separate, so you can see exactly what the ads cost.

      
What kinds of businesses do you work with?
Local service businesses: HVAC, plumbing, roofing, electrical, landscaping and similar trades that win work from nearby homeowners.
