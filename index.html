01  /  About

      
Media buyer by trade. Systems builder by habit.

      
I started in paid media in 2018, running direct-response campaigns and building customer acquisition for companies in competitive categories. Since then I've managed seven-figure monthly ad budgets, built the funnels behind them, led creative teams, and run campaigns across social, search and video.

      
My work has spanned fintech, financial publishing, precious metals, insurance, healthcare, home services, e-commerce and subscription businesses. The common thread: find what converts, prove it with numbers, then scale it.

      
Today I also build. I use AI coding agents to turn the repetitive parts of media buying into software: creative production, campaign monitoring, budget guardrails and reporting. Quorum is where that work is going.
